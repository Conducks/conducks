### **S E C T I O N  0 1**

**Project Overview**

– What it is: Conducks is a local-first, Git-native structural intelligence platform for engineers. It maps codebases into a deterministic graph (the Synapse) and provides architectural analysis, governance enforcement, and impact assessment via CLI, MCP tools, and a visual dashboard (the Mirror).

– What stage it's at: Built and functional. v1.0.0 released. Open source. Pre-public launch — in the phase of refining identity before growth push.

– What exists already: A functional product, a GitHub repo, a working web dashboard (the Mirror), and a CLI. No public brand identity beyond a name and a dark UI.

– What is needed from the designer: Brand identity (logo, wordmark, color system), GitHub profile assets, social media templates, and marketing materials for developer communities (HN, Reddit, Twitter/X).

– Primary market: Senior engineers and AI engineering teams at companies with 10k–1M+ LOC codebases.

### **S E C T I O N  0 3**

**Target Audience**

**1. Primary Users — Senior Engineers / AI-aware Teams**
– Who they are: Staff+ engineers, platform engineers, engineering leads at companies where AI coding agents are already in use.
– Age range: 28–45
– Needs: Structural accuracy when agents navigate large codebases. Confidence that refactoring won't break unknown callers. Visibility into technical debt.
– Pain points: AI agents hallucinate codebase structure. grep/RAG is probabilistic. No tool gives a proven map of what calls what.

**2. Secondary Users — Architects and Tech Leads**
– Who they are: CTOs, principal engineers who need architectural governance and drift detection.
– Motivation: Enforce structural laws across teams. Detect when the codebase drifts from intended architecture.

**3. B2B (Future)**
– Company type: Scale-up to enterprise (Series B+), 20–500 engineers.
– Decision makers: Head of Engineering, Platform Lead, DevTools budget owner.

**Psychographics (All Audiences)**
– Values: Precision over approximation. Proven over probable. Ownership over dependency on external services.
– Behaviors: Read technical papers, follow Hacker News closely, distrust black boxes, prefer open-source first.
– What they trust: Mathematical rigor, clear explanations, tools that show their work.
– What they avoid: Marketing language, vague AI claims, anything that requires cloud access for basic functionality.

### **S E C T I O N  0 4**

**Brand Identity**

**Brand Personality**
– Tone: Precise, architectural, confident. Like a senior engineer who has seen everything and explains it clearly.
– Voice: Direct. No filler. Technical but never obscure. Metric-forward ("Gravity: 0.89, Risk: 0.73").
– Feeling: The feeling of finally seeing your codebase as a map. Clarity. Control.
– Not: Startup hype. Friendly chat assistant. Over-designed or decorative. Corporate blue.

**Typography**
– Primary font: A geometric monospace for headings and metrics (JetBrains Mono, or similar).
– Secondary font: Clean sans-serif for body and UI labels (Inter, Geist).
– Special: The wordmark should feel structural — like code architecture made visual.

**Color Palette**
– Primary: Deep void black `#0a0a0f` — the canvas.
– Structural accent: Electric teal / cyan `#00d4ff` — the "photon" of the Kinetic Mirror.
– Risk gradient: Teal → Amber → Red (low risk to critical). Never pastel.
– Text: Off-white `#e8e8f0`. Muted secondary `#888899`.
– Avoid: Blues and greens that read as "safe/healthy corporate". This is physics, not a dashboard startup.

**Design Principles**
– Layout: Sparse. Let space breathe. The data is the hero, not the chrome.
– Structural rhythm: Elements align to an invisible grid like nodes in a force-directed graph.
– Components: Terminal-native aesthetic — monospaced metrics, badge labels, thin borders.
– Visual style: Dark editorial. Scientific instrument. Not gaming, not fintech.

### **S E C T I O N  0 6**

**Content Direction**

**Key Messages**
– "Your codebase has physics. Conducks reads them." (Gravity, Entropy, Risk)
– "Zero hallucinations. Proven structural paths."
– "Not search. Structure."
– "Give your AI agents a map, not a guess."

**Do**
– Show real numbers and metrics in visuals.
– Use graph/network imagery that feels mathematical, not decorative.
– Lean into the dark terminal aesthetic.
– Show the structural layers as something beautiful and legible.

**Don't**
– Use the word "AI" in isolation — it reads as another AI tool. Say "deterministic", "graph-proven", "structural".
– Use blue/green corporate SaaS color schemes.
– Show code snippets as the hero — the graph is the hero.
– Use rounded, friendly shapes — this is an instrument, not a consumer app.
