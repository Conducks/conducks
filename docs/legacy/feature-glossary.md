- **Purpose**: Index the whole project into a structural graph.
- **Mechanics**: Parallel Wasm induction wave based on your `HEAD` commit.
- **Resonance**: Transforms files into nodes and calls into edges.

### `conducks query`
- **Purpose**: Precise symbol location.
- **Mechanics**: Fuzzy match or regex lookup against the **Atom Map**.
- **Resonance**: Returns unique Synapse IDs (e.g. `src/lib/foo.ts::Bar`).

- **Purpose**: High-level structural health manifest.
- **Mechanics**: Aggregates graph-wide metrics to find **Pillars** and **Hotspots**.
- **Resonance**: Instantly identifies "God Objects" or critical entry points.
