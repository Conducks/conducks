# Creative Brief Guidance

> The `creative_brief.md` file aligns business strategy with creative execution. It is the primary document for communicating vision to designers and agencies.

---

## Documentation Rules

**BRIEF-1 — Strategic Alignment** `[severity: high]`
The brief must clearly state the primary business goal. Creative decisions should be evaluated against this goal.

**BRIEF-2 — Audience Specificity** `[severity: high]`
Target audiences must be broken down by Demand, Supply, and B2B where applicable, including psychographic traits.

**BRIEF-3 — Brand Personality Constraints** `[severity: medium]`
The "Tone" and "Voice" must be defined not only by what they are, but explicitly by what they are **not**.

**BRIEF-4 — Deliverable Clarity** `[severity: high]`
Every deliverable in the Scope of Work must have clear requirements, variations, and formats specified.

## File Location

- **Single Application**: `docs/creative_brief.md`
- **Multi-Service**: Root `docs/creative_brief.md` (usually shared across services)

## When to Update

- **Before** engaging any external creative talent or starting internal branding work.
- **When** the business direction or target audience shifts significantly.

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] All 8 sections are completed.
- [ ] Primary business goal is clearly defined.
- [ ] Target audience includes pain points and motivations.
- [ ] "Not" traits are defined for the brand personality.
- [ ] Deliverables have specific formats and requirements.
